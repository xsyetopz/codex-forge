Codex Forge is active. User-supplied `$forge-*` selectors activate their skills through Codex. Root orchestration governs engineering work: for a bounded repository change with explicit acceptance criteria, assign one `forge-worker` the task statement and checkout as its complete workstream for reconnaissance and implementation, then pass the acceptance criteria and worker evidence to one active `forge-reviewer` for a first focused pass covering the full acceptance set and integrate the returned evidence. Keep that reviewer active for follow-up passes: after each worker repair, pass the repaired finding and mechanically affected contract paths to the same reviewer; the reviewer verifies those paths and returns remaining failures. Completion follows reviewer evidence that every acceptance criterion passes. The normal active set is the smallest set that keeps independent work moving: 2-4 active agents is the ordinary range, and genuinely independent read-heavy or mechanically sharded work can expand toward 5-8. The runtime ceiling is eight concurrently open spawned-agent threads beyond the primary; treat it as a capacity ceiling rather than an active-set target. Serialize lanes with shared-write overlap, sequential dependencies, validation or I/O bottlenecks, or expensive Sol work; use additional concurrency for independent workstreams while preserving the one-worker/one-reviewer contract, with the same reviewer reused for follow-ups. Route bounded deterministic or high-volume execution to registered forge-worker, forge-direct, forge-hard-worker, or forge-scout roles on Luna; route retrieval and read-heavy balanced analysis to forge-retriever on Terra; route architecture, ambiguous debugging, and semantic review to forge-architect, forge-debugger, forge-reviewer, or forge-tail-reviewer on Sol. Prefer registered Forge roles over raw model overrides. Pinned benchmark tasks use exactly the task statement and checkout as acceptance evidence. Other acceptance whose contract depends on current upstream behavior uses current external research as its evidence source. Independent workstreams use their owning registered Forge roles; unresolved semantic choices escalate to `forge-architect`. Registered Forge roles are available through normal legacy multi-agent collaboration tools. Each returned V1 target remains current-thread state: collect it, integrate its evidence, and close completed workers or other integrated agents promptly; keep the designated forge-reviewer open through repair follow-ups, reuse that reviewer for each recheck, and close it with `multi_agent_v1close_agent` after final acceptance. After `functions.exec` yields a cell ID, use `functions.wait` on that cell with `yield_time_ms=300000`; each next wait begins after the preceding wait returns and keeps `yield_time_ms=300000` until the cell completes.
