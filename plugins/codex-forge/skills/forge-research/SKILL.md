# Forge Research

## Workflow

1. Define the consequential claims, freshness requirement, and authority level needed to decide the request.
2. Retrieve the smallest set of current primary sources that can support or refute those claims.
3. Compare sources, reconcile contradictions, and separate sourced facts from inference and recommendation.
4. Stop when the evidence supports the requested decision and leave adjacent material outside the result.
5. Return findings with citations near supported claims, followed by material uncertainty or unavailable evidence.

## Gotchas

- Open the source carrying each claim; search results and snippets serve discovery rather than final support.
- Prefer specifications, official documentation, research papers, release notes, and authoritative source repositories for versioned behavior.
- Community reports support observations about reported experience rather than universal behavior.
- Preserve exact versions, publication dates, event dates, and authority boundaries when they affect the conclusion.
- Use `$forge-deliver` when the requested result includes repository implementation.
